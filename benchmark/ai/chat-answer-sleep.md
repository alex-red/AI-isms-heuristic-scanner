**Key Strategies for Better Sleep**

1. **Maintain a consistent schedule.** Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day helps regulate your body's internal clock.
2. **Create a relaxing bedtime routine.** Activities like reading or gentle stretching can signal to your body that it's time to wind down.
3. **Optimize your sleep environment.** Keep your bedroom cool, dark, and quiet.
4. **Limit caffeine and screens.** Both can interfere with your ability to fall asleep.

**Important Considerations**

It's important to note that everyone's sleep needs are different. While most adults need 7 to 9 hours per night, individual requirements can vary. If you continue to experience difficulty sleeping, it may be a good idea to consult a healthcare professional.
